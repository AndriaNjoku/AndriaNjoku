I build software that makes complicated things easier to understand—from what happens in Parliament to what an AI agent is doing on your computer.

My foundation is native Android: Kotlin, Jetpack Compose and the details that make an interface feel right. My work now reaches across mobile, web, backend services and developer tools. I care about the experience someone touches and the systems they need to trust underneath it.

## Politics, with the record attached

**[Karma Politics](https://karmapolitics.co.uk)** brings UK parliamentary activity closer to the people it affects: follow representatives, explore votes and debates, and look at the evidence behind political claims.

I'm building across the product, from the Android interface to the services that turn parliamentary records into something people can navigate. Recent work includes a shared React Native experience for mobile and web, still in development.

> Less “trust me.” More “show me the record.”

## Tools for working with AI

**Nudge by Notch** is my work on coding-agent supervision: making agent activity visible, engineering checks inspectable and corrections easier to follow. It's an evolving developer tool, grounded in the practical work of building with agents.

**[OmiDaemon](https://github.com/AndriaNjoku/OmiDaemon)** explores a different problem: what happens after a wearable produces a transcript? This open-source project turns that material into structured notes, tasks and follow-up drafts, with Obsidian integration.
